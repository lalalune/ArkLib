# An explicit differential interpolation kernel beyond Johnson

The [scalar differential-carrier argument](astra_scalar_differential_carrier-2026-09-05.md)
now has a complete finite construction check: an actual interpolation
polynomial, every node's contact equations, the entire decoding list, and
the rational Taylor reconstruction of all its members. Four closed finite
predicates are also checked by the Lean kernel. This is a small-instance
certificate, not a formalization of the general production theorem or an
MCA bound.

## The exact instance

Work over F17 on nodes 0,...,12, with polynomial degree at most w=2 and
agreement target A=5. The received word, in node order, is

```text
[0,0,0,0,0,8,2,15,13,13,3,7,11].
```

This is strictly beyond the ordinary Johnson agreement threshold, because
`A^2=25<26=n*w`. Use contact order m=4, weighted cap D=m*A=20, and
R exponent cap two. The source space is

```text
span { X^a Y^i R^j : a+2i+j<20, j<=2 }.
```

For each node x impose vanishing of all terms of contact weight less than
four after `X=x+t, Y=v(x)+t*R+z`, where t has weight one and z weight two.

| Quantity | Result |
|---|---:|
| Source coefficients | 300 |
| Exact single-node rank | 23 |
| Guaranteed global nullity | 300-13*23=1 |
| Measured complete global rank over F17 | 295 |
| Actual global kernel dimension | 5 |
| Nonzero terms of retained Q | 249 |
| Actual total Y,R degree of Q | 10 |

The [stored coefficient certificate](../../scripts/probes/fixtures/astra_scalar_kernel_f17.json)
uses entries `[X exponent,Y exponent,R exponent,coefficient mod17]`.
It is regenerated by column elimination with exact combination tracking;
it is not a presumed solution supplied to the solver. Its normalized JSON
SHA-256 is

```text
8c68d8b3268f0ef2b8b5097686d663cac641674467a491df00a4c9e1cc6b1615
```

With the same D and m, the complete maps for R caps zero and one have
ranks 110/110 and 210/210. Thus neither smaller source space has a nonzero
interpolant for this particular received word. This is a finite comparison
between the stated spaces, not a claim excluding every alternative source.

## Independent expansion, census, and reconstruction

After constructing Q using low-weight multinomial matrix entries, the
checker expands the full polynomial substitution separately, without contact
truncation, at each of the 13 nodes. Every coefficient of weight below four
is zero, and Q itself is nonzero. The degree in Y,R is ten, below the
characteristic, and w=2 is also below the characteristic.

An exhaustive evaluation of all 17^3=4913 quadratics gives exactly

```text
f0=0,
f1=X^2,
f2=7+15X+12X^2.
```

For every member, direct polynomial substitution verifies Q(X,f,f')=0
and H(X,f,f')!=0, where H=Q_R. Put G=-Q_X-R*Q_Y. The three reconstruction
coefficients, with common denominator H, have numerators

```text
H*Y-X*H*R+(X^2/2)*G,
H*R-X*G,
G/2.
```

Their specializations are exactly H times the three coefficients of f.
The nine identities are checked as univariate polynomial identities over
F17, not merely by evaluating at the 17 field elements. The differential
identity G(X,f,f')=f''*H(X,f,f') is checked separately for each candidate.

## What Lean checks

The standalone [Lean certificate](../../scripts/probes/astra_scalar_kernel_witness.lean)
imports only Std and contains the same 249 coefficient rows. Its four
theorems use ordinary `by decide`:

* `supported_nonzero` checks the weighted support, R cap, total Y,R cap,
  and the actual coefficient of X*Y^7*R^2, equal to one modulo 17.
* `all_contacts` checks every low-contact-weight coefficient at all 13 nodes.
* `complete_quadratic_list` checks every coefficient triple in F17^3 against
  the agreement predicate and the displayed three-element list.
* `finite_parameters` checks the strict Johnson comparison, characteristic
  inequalities, and numerical nullity lower bound.

All four compiled with Lean 4.30.0-rc2 and reported **no axioms** under
`#print axioms`. No `native_decide`, `sorry`, or extra axiom is used.
The Python checker also confirms that the Lean coefficient rows match the
stored certificate exactly.

These are closed finite predicates. The general correspondence between the
contact predicate and polynomial interpolation, the general carrier-degree
argument, the production-size source kernel, and the MCA/ProtocolClaim
bridge are not formalized by these four theorems. The numerical rank 295
and the symbolic reconstruction identities are Python checks, separate
from the Lean results above.
