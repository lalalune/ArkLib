# Proper cuts from a bound on the acceleration's field degree

There is a sufficient condition for surface properness of an untrimmed
second-Hasse source. Normalize the received direction to be nonzero at
every node. On an irreducible factor F, let a denote the rational expression
for the second Hasse derivative. If the degree of the line parameter Z over
the field generated by a exceeds the source's Z-degree cap, then some
member of the **full** nonzero source kernel has a proper pullback on F.

At the binding actual degree flag, this proves properness for the T=1042
and T=1031 sources **if a has algebraic degree at most two over
K(X,Y,R)**. This hypothesis is not established for general binding factors.
The result does not prove general surface properness, improve a prize
allowance, or complete the phase recurrence. The proof below is written;
exact finite controls passed, but independent review and Lean integration
remain outstanding.

## Normalizing the received direction

Let the n nodes be distinct and let |K|>n. Choose c in K different from
every u1_i. Replace

```text
u1_i by u1_i-c,
f(X) by f(X)-gamma*c,
Q(X,Y,R,S,Z) by Q(X,Y+c*Z,R,S,Z).
```

The polynomial degree bound on f and every agreement are preserved; a
constant polynomial is a codeword. The inverse uses -c. First and second
derivatives of f are unchanged. The transformation preserves the full box

```text
weightedDegree_(1,w,w-1,w-2,0)(Q) < D,
degree_(Y,R,S,Z)(Q) <= T,
degree_R(Q) <= s1, degree_S(Q) <= s2,
```

for w>=2. Substitution of Y+cZ cannot increase any of these degrees, and
the inverse has the same property. The local contact substitutions agree
exactly: `(u1_i-c)*Z+c*Z=u1_i*Z`. Hence this is an isomorphism of the full
contact kernels, including their weighted and residual degree filtrations.
It also preserves the first-order cumulative R, YR and total degree flags.

The following arguments take place **after this one normalization**, with
u1_i nonzero at every node. Algebraic degrees of the acceleration must also
be measured in that coordinate system. They are not claimed invariant
under an arbitrary change of coordinates.

The [generic-jet repair](astra_acceleration_chart-2026-09-05.md) subsequently
weakens this restriction: the criterion also holds in any unchanged
coordinate system with at most w-2 zero direction entries. This matters
because a constant shift can turn rational acceleration into full algebraic
degree. At the companion n,w, at most two constants violate this weaker
zero-count condition; the low-degree acceleration condition is still a
separate requirement.

## No parameter-independent universal factor

Let V be a nonzero full second-Hasse contact kernel in the displayed box,
with contact at least m at every node. Suppose
G in K[X,Y,R,S] has positive S degree and is primitive as a polynomial in S
over K[X,Y,R]. Then G cannot divide every member of V.

Primitivity implies that X-x_i does not divide G. Thus
G(x_i,Y,R,S) is a nonzero polynomial. Since u1_i is nonzero, substitution
`Y=u0_i+u1_i*Z` is an injective affine change of its Y variable. In
particular,

```text
G(x_i,u0_i+u1_i*Z,R,S) != 0.
```

This is the contact-weight-zero part of G under
`Y=u0_i+u1_i*Z+tR-t^2*S+v`, with weights t=1,v=3. Therefore G has contact
order zero at every node.

If G divided all of V, choose a nonzero Q in V of minimum residual total
degree and write Q=G*H. Contact orders add under multiplication, so H still
has contact at least m at every node. All four box degrees add under
multiplication of nonzero polynomials. Consequently H is still in the
same box, and its residual total degree is strictly smaller because G
has positive S degree. This contradicts the choice of Q.

This is the [full-kernel descent argument](astra_kernel_descent_2026-09-04.md)
with a new way to obtain contact order zero. It is not valid for an
arbitrary projected subspace unless that subspace is separately shown
closed under the replacement Q/G. Primitivity also matters: a nonconstant
factor in X can vanish at a node even when u1_i is nonzero.

## Acceleration-extension criterion

Assume char(K)!=2. Let F in K[X,Y,R,Z] be irreducible, with positive
Z degree d and nonzero F_R. Set

```text
K0 = K(X,Y,R),
L  = K0[Z]/(F),
a  = -(F_X+R*F_Y)/(2*F_R) in L,
E  = K0(a),
rho = [E:K0], e = [L:E], so d=rho*e.
```

Irreducibility and positive Z degree make L a field of degree d over K0.
The image of F_R is nonzero: otherwise irreducibility, localization, and
the strict drop of R degree under differentiation would give F|F_R.

Let V be as above, or more generally let T_Z be any upper bound on the
Z degree of all its members. If

```text
e > T_Z,                                                  (1)
```

then there is a Q in V whose cleared pullback is not divisible by F.

**Proof.** Suppose instead that every Q vanishes on the regular F surface
after replacing S by a. Write

```text
Q = sum_(j=0..T_Z) q_j(X,Y,R,S)*Z^j.
```

The coefficients q_j(a) lie in E. Since L=E(Z), the degree of Z over E is
e. Condition (1) makes `1,Z,...,Z^T_Z` linearly independent over E. Thus
q_j(a)=0 for every j and every Q in V.

Let g(S) in K0[S] be the minimal polynomial of a. Clear denominators and
remove coefficient content to obtain a primitive polynomial
G in K[X,Y,R,S]. It has positive S degree rho. Each q_j is divisible by g
over K0[S], and Gauss's lemma makes it divisible by G in
K[X,Y,R,S]. Hence G divides every Q in V. This contradicts the preceding
parameter-independent-factor argument. This proves (1).

No separability assumption is needed for the field-degree or independence
steps. Denominator clearing by `(2F_R)^s2` is legitimate on the regular
surface. Nonvanishing in L means precisely that the resulting polynomial
is not divisible by F. For geometric components, apply the criterion to
the actual component and its degrees over the relevant coefficient field;
do not silently reuse the degree of a larger reducible union.

One convenient sufficient form of (1) is

```text
d > rho*T_Z.                                             (2)
```

Conversely, if the entire nonzero full kernel is contained after pullback,
then necessarily `e<=T_Z`, and hence `rho*T_Z>=d`.
This is a restriction on the extension degree, not an assertion that the
remaining high-degree cases cannot occur.

## Binding source consequences, conditional on the field degree

After normalization, an F of **actual** residual total degree 2364 and YR
degree at most 47 still has

```text
d=degree_Z(F) >= 2364-47 = 2317.
```

Indeed, a monomial of maximum residual total degree has Z exponent at
least 2364-47. An upper bound on total degree without attainment is
insufficient for this argument.

Apply (2) to the
[four positive full source certificates](astra_hasse_rank_profile-2026-09-05.md):

| m | S cap | Total cap T | Sufficient acceleration degree | Cleared cut total cap |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 80 | 6 | 1042 | rho<=2 | 15220 |
| 99 | 8 | 1031 | rho<=2 | 19935 |
| 99 | 1 | 4156 | None from d>=2317 alone | 6519 |
| 99 | 2 | 2270 | rho=1 | 6996 |

The first two rows use `2*1042<2317` and `2*1031<2317`. Thus a factor with
rational or quadratic algebraic acceleration has a proper extra cut from
either of these sources. Their degree caps fit the earlier
[conditional component split](astra_hasse_component_split-2026-09-05.md).
This handles that additional factor class; it does not prove the
acceleration has degree at most two on the binding factor, and does not
handle all other maxima in the phase recurrence.

The high-parameter-degree variant in the
[containment construction](astra_hasse_containment-2026-09-05.md) is
consistent with this criterion. Its primitive equation is

```text
(L0*J-W*W')Y+(W^2-L0*W*W')R+L0*W^2*S-J*Z=0,
J=(W')^2-W*W''/2, L0=X-xi.
```

Here L0 denotes the linear polynomial from that construction, not the
extension field L. Since J is nonzero at every simple root of W, the
acceleration is affine in Z with nonzero coefficient J/(L0*W^2). Thus Z
is recovered from a over K0: E=L, e=1 and rho=d. A high degree of F in Z
alone does not imply a small rho or a large relative degree e.

The [positive full-kernel field-degree controls](astra_acceleration_chart-2026-09-05.md)
give an additional limitation: an actual irreducible universal first-order
factor with a positive uniform margin and a regular MCA-bad selected seed
can have rho=d=6 and e=1. These controls use nine nodes, including at the
companion characteristic. They do not realize the binding production flag,
but show that the low-degree hypothesis is not automatic from those
provenance conditions.

## Exact finite controls

Run:

```sh
python3 scripts/probes/astra_acceleration_extension_check.py
```

The checker uses only the Python standard library. Over each of F17,
F257 and F2130706433 it verifies the normalization and its inverse on all
447 monomials of a source box, with 2682 exact column/node contact
identities per field. This includes an original direction with a zero
coordinate.

Two generic surfaces provide rational and quadratic acceleration controls:

```text
F1=R-Y*Z^4,          a1=R^2/(2Y),
G1=2Y*S-R^2;

F2=R-Y*Z^4-Z^8,      a2=(R/2)*Z^4,
G2=4S^2+2Y*R*S-R^3.
```

Each F is irreducible, being monic linear in R; Gauss's lemma then also
gives irreducibility over K(X,Y,R) as a polynomial in Z. Their Z degrees
are four and eight. For F2, Z^4 satisfies an irreducible quadratic over
K(X,Y,R): its discriminant Y^2+4R is not a square in that rational
function field. Thus rho=2 and e=4; for F1, rho=1 and e=4.

In each finite field the checker finds a Y,R specialization where the Z
polynomial remains irreducible, and checks irreducibility with Rabin's
criterion. The twelve images `Z^j*a^k`, 0<=j<=3,0<=k<=2, have rank four
for F1 and rank eight for F2, as predicted by their acceleration degrees.
At the excluded degree boundary, `R*Z^4-2S` vanishes for F2 despite having
S degree below that of G2. This checks why the strict inequality e>T_Z
is used in the field-independence step; it is not a full-kernel
counterexample at equality.

A separate full-kernel calculation uses

```text
n=6,w=2,A=5,m=3,D=15,T=3,s1=3,s2=2,
nodes=0,...,5,
u0=(0,0,0,0,0,1), u1_i=1/(i+1).
```

In all three fields there are 447 columns, local rank 58, and global rank
348. Both the uniform margin and the actual nullity equal 99. Every basis
vector is checked directly against the full matrix. For both F1 and F2,
the checker exhibits a regular surface point where a kernel vector's
pullback is nonzero. These are finite properness controls, **not** examples
of a universal old factor or a realization of the binding degree flag.

Finally, `2X^2*S-X*R+Y` has contact order two at node zero with u0=u1=0,
but order zero there with u0=0,u1=1. This checks that the no-zero-direction
step in the simple descent proof cannot be omitted.

No new Lean theorem or prize score is asserted by this checker.
